11

Bakers Lane, Epping - final phase nearing completion

Roadworks - nearly finished

Epping

The long-running resurfacing and improvement works on Bakers Lane are in their third and final stage. The latest update from the council's roads team says footway resurfacing is complete and electricians are connecting the new street lighting, so this looks close to wrapping up, though no exact reopening date has been confirmed yet.

Source: Bakers Lane progress update

12

Loughton water main replacement - work continues

Ongoing - multi-year project

Loughton

This multi-year project to replace 19 kilometres of ageing water pipe across around 60 Loughton streets is continuing. Work on Queens Road, Spareleaze Hill, Hillyfields, Woodbury Hill and Rectory Lane has finished, and the project is now moving on to other streets, with the whole scheme expected to run into 2027.

Source: Thames Water: High Beech mains replacement

13

South Woodford burst water main - supplies restored

Mostly resolved

Woodford Green

A burst water main on Chigwell Road in mid-August cut off water to thousands of homes across the area. Water supplies were restored within about a week, and the council said the road itself was expected to reopen soon after. We haven't found a more recent update since, but nothing suggests it's still affected.

Source: Redbridge Council: water supplies restored
