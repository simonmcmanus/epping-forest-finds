Privacy & Location Data

To enable your location we need your consent to collect anonymous usage data that helps improve the app:

  • GPS position — updated roughly once per minute while the app is open
  • Direction & navigation — which features you navigate toward
  • Interactions — which trees, paths, and places you tap on

You are identified only by a random anonymous ID — no name, email, or account is required. Your data will never be sold individually.

Anonymised data may be used in the future to show relevant suggestions or advertising. You can withdraw consent at any time via Settings → Privacy.
