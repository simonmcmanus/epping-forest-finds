Good to know

Does it really work without a phone signal?
Yes. Once downloaded, the map, trees and paths work offline. Fresh cow locations need a connection; offline, the map shows their last saved positions.
Is it free?
Yes. Free to use, with no account needed.
Where does the tree data come from?
Our tree data comes from the Epping Forest Veteran Tree Register, provided by the City of London. We know some of the data may not be completely accurate. As part of the trial, we’re working to understand the issues and improve the data, and we’ll report our findings back to the City of London. Paths, pubs, car parks and transport come from OpenStreetMap contributors.
How do you know where the cattle are?
GPS collars report their positions through Nofence. The map refreshes them periodically while online. Offline, you see their last saved positions, which may be out of date.
Which area does it cover?
Epping Forest and nearby pubs, stations and facilities within walking distance.
