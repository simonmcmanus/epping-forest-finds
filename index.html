Works where your phone doesn't

Get the map downloaded while you’ve got wi-fi or a bit of signal. Then off you go. The trees, paths and landmarks stay on your phone, even when the bars disappear. Which, round here, they do.

Get to know the old trees

All 24,906 trees from the Epping Forest Veteran Tree Register, right here on the map with their species and records. Got a tag number? Pop it in to find that tree and see its estimated age. There’s a fair bit of history in those trunks.

Follow the longhorns

The longhorns have got GPS collars. Very high-tech for a bunch that spends most of the day chewing. We show their latest reported positions and check online for updates now and then. Offline, you’ll see the last saved positions, so they might have wandered off since.

What’s with the tree tags?

Spotted a little numbered tag on a tree and wondered what it’s doing there? That’s its number in the Veteran Tree Register. Pop it in and have a nose, or pick a tree to go and find next time you’re out.

No signal? Here’s the plan

Get it downloaded before you head out. Your signal can suit itself after that.

  1. Open it onceSomewhere with a decent signal or wi‑fi.
  2. It downloadsThe whole forest saves to your device — a one-time wait.
  3. Walk out of rangeThe map, your position and your heading all carry on working.
A note on the moving herd

Fresh cow locations need a connection. The app checks for updates periodically; offline positions may be out of date.

Good to know

Does it really work without a phone signal?
Yes. Once downloaded, the map, trees and paths work offline. Fresh cow locations need a connection; offline, the map shows their last saved positions.
Is it free?
Yep. Free to use, and no account to faff about with.
Where does the tree data come from?
The Epping Forest Veteran Tree Register. Paths, pubs, car parks and transport come from OpenStreetMap contributors.
How do you know where the cattle are?
The cattle wear GPS collars. Their positions come through the Nofence open data API, which we check when the map loads and now and then while you use it. They do like a wander. Offline, you see their last saved positions, which may be out of date.
Which area does it cover?
Epping Forest and the bits within walking distance around it. The pub or station just outside the trees is on here too. You’ve got to get home somehow.
